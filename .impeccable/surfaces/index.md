panel grid: no card, no shadow, no corner radius, no KPI strip. Structure is carried by
hairline rules, register marks, and column discipline — the furniture of a statistical
abstract — because the product's claim is that every number is reproducible and sourced,
and that is what a reference volume's page already looks like.

marks at plate corners and an 8px dot matrix filling structural voids. One grotesque,

Critical `#4A1A18`.
